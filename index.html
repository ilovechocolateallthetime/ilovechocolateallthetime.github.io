SO

Student Organization (SO)

The Student Organization (SO) represents all students at Francis Lewis High School. We organize school events, spirit weeks, fundraisers, and help connect students with clubs like Key Club, Choir, and our sports teams.

What does SO do?

  • Plans school dances, spirit days, and Pep Rallies.
  • Supports all 8 school clubs and sports teams.
  • Listens to student ideas to make our school better.
